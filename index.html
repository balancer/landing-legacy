BIP-928 ↗

Balancer is winding down

BAL holders approved BIP-928 on 29 September 2026. The contracts are non-custodial, so you can always withdraw your funds, whether or not Balancer keeps operating. The treasury goes to BAL holders, who burn their BAL to redeem it.

30 October 2026: pools move to withdrawals only, and bug bounty coverage ends. Withdrawals stay open.
Since 30 October 2026, pools are withdrawals only and bug bounty coverage has ended. v3 pools kept running for partners stay live until 30 November 2026, when the v3 Vault is paused.
All pools are withdrawals only, and your funds remain withdrawable. Round one of the treasury redemption opens at the end of May 2027.
Round one of the treasury redemption is open until the end of November 2027. Only addresses that redeem in round one share in round two.

What this means for you

Liquidity providers

  • Your funds stay withdrawable, and withdrawing never depends on Balancer.
  • From 30 Oct 2026, pools that can be paused are withdrawals only. The rest keep working, with the protocol fee set to zero where the contracts allow it.
  • v3 pools kept running for partners are paused on 30 Nov 2026.
  • Use the withdraw app or the manual exit guide.

BAL holders

  • From the end of May 2027 you have six months to burn your BAL and receive your pro-rata share of the treasury in kind.
  • Round two is airdropped within two months of the close, but only to addresses that redeemed in round one, in proportion to what they redeemed.
  • A final sweep follows six months later, to the same addresses.
  • BAL holdings from the Treasury will not be re-distributed.

veBAL holders

  • Every veBAL lock that existed on 14 Sep 2026 expires before round one opens.
  • Your lock unlocks into 80/20 BAL/WETH BPT. Exit it to BAL with the veBAL unlock app, then redeem.
  • A lock extended after 14 Sep 2026 redeems only if it unlocks within the window.

auraBAL, sdBAL and tetuBAL

  • auraBAL and sdBAL unwind on their own protocols' calendars. Exit to BAL and redeem like any other holder. A position not unwound to BAL by the end of round one does not redeem.
  • tetuBAL never becomes BAL. Holders on record at the 14 Sep 2026 block receive BAL from the treasury on Ethereum, equal to 50% of the BAL behind their tetuBAL, when round one opens. They then redeem it like any other BAL.

LPs affected by the attacks

  • Recovered funds belong to the LPs of the affected pools. They stay outside the treasury distribution.
  • Currently there are no distribution plans for unclaimed assets recovered; a separate BIP needs to come forward for how to handle dormant assets.

Key dates

  1. 29 Sep 2026 BIP-928 passed, with tetuBAL at 50%
  2. 30 Oct 2026 Pools move to withdrawals only. Bug bounty coverage ends
  3. 30 Nov 2026 v3 Vault paused. The balancer.fi app is discontinued
  4. End Feb 2027 Implementation spec for the claim contract published for comment
  5. End May 2027 Round one opens: burn BAL and receive the bulk of the treasury. The snapshot block is announced at least two weeks ahead
  6. End Nov 2027 Round one closes. The record of who redeemed becomes final
  7. End Jan 2028 Round two airdrop to the addresses that redeemed
  8. End Jul 2028 Final sweep to the same addresses. The process ends
